✨ What Arivu can do

• Schedule appointments from a sentence — it understands dates & times in English and Tamil.

📱 On your iPhone

Add Arivu to your Home Screen to use it full-screen like an app:
  1. Save this file (or open it in Safari).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Arivu from the icon — it runs full-screen, no browser bars.
🎤 Voice input and sound need a tap first (Apple’s rule). Tap anywhere once, then talk.

ℹ️ Help

How does the call demo work?

The call screen opens and Arivu speaks Tamil through your speaker. You act as the person who answers: tap Yes / No / Another time (or type/speak). The appointment status changes to confirmed, cancelled, or rescheduled and the whole call is saved in the Call log.

Can it ring a real phone number?

Not yet — that needs a telephone provider such as Twilio, and the Tamil audio can be served from the clips already inside this app. Ask and it can be wired up.

Nothing speaks when I open it?

Apple blocks sound until you touch the screen once: tap Play voice or anywhere, then it works. Also check the side switch isn’t on silent.
